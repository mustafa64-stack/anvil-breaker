The 15-Minute Protocol Architecture

Four distinct cognitive phases designed to systematically eliminate tutorial hell, prevent ego-traps, and rewire interview problem solving.

PHASE 1 00–03m

Read & Trace

Hands off the keyboard. Dissect constraints, identify invariants, and trace 2 custom edge cases on pen and paper.

PHASE 2 03–07m

Pick a Pattern

Map to known paradigms: Two Pointers, Monotonic Stack, Sliding Window, BFS/DFS, or DP. Formulate brute-force baseline.

PHASE 3 07–13m

Code & Test

Write clean production-ready code. No copy-pasting, no AI autocomplete crutches. Dry-run sample edge cases before submitting.

PHASE 4 13–15m

Cut Your Losses (Rule 2)

Hard stop. If stuck at minute 13, open hints, understand the mathematical proof, and re-implement from scratch from memory.
